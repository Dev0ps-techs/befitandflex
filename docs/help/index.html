Help

Delivery, returns & contact

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across the UAE, and internationally on request. Delivery options and costs are shown at checkout.

How do I choose my size?

Use our Size Guide. If you are between sizes, message us on WhatsApp and we will help you choose.

Can I return or exchange?

Unworn items with tags can be exchanged or returned within 14 days of delivery. See our returns policy at checkout for full details.

How do I care for my activewear?

Wash cold, inside out, with similar colours. Do not tumble dry or iron the logo. Air dry flat to keep the shape and stretch.
